## 🗺️ Selected Work

### ⛰️ [Mountain Road Geotechnical Hazard Assessment](https://github.com/oscgonz19/mountain-road-hazard-dashboard)

Applied geoscience and risk assessment for mountain infrastructure in the Colombian Andes.

Combines:

`Field Geology` · `Geotechnics` · `Google Earth Engine` · `Remote Sensing` · `GIS` · `Streamlit`

- Geological and hydrogeological interpretation
- Factor of Safety–based hazard classification
- 30+ years of Landsat/Sentinel vegetation analysis
- Spatial interpolation and hazard zonation
- Interactive decision-support dashboard

---

### 🛰️ [Vegetation Change Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/oscgonz19/vegetation-change-platform)

A Google Earth Engine–based system for detecting environmental change across decades of satellite imagery.

`Python` · `Google Earth Engine` · `Landsat` · `Sentinel-2` · `GeoPandas` · `Streamlit`

Processes multi-sensor satellite archives to detect vegetation loss, recovery and landscape transformation using spectral indices such as NDVI, NBR, NDWI and EVI.

---

### 🎨 [ElevToposter](https://github.com/oscgonz19/elevtoposter)

Experimental project combining **digital elevation models, cartography and generative visual design**.

`Python` · `SRTM` · `Rasterio` · `NumPy` · `Matplotlib`

Turns elevation data into minimalist terrain posters using hillshade, contour lines, hypsometric bands and custom visual themes.

> Part cartography, part scientific visualization, part experiment in representing landscapes as data-driven art.

---

### 🏔️ [Expedition Mapper](https://github.com/oscgonz19/montania-expedition-mapper)

A cartographic toolkit for turning expedition GPS tracks into terrain-aware maps.

`Python` · `GeoPandas` · `Rasterio` · `DEM` · `GPX/KMZ` · `Streamlit`

Includes:

- Elevation profiles
- Hillshade terrain
- Contour generation
- Route statistics
- Slope analysis
- Waypoint processing
- Expedition map export

---

### 🧠 [Bayesian Security Anomaly Detection](https://github.com/oscgonz19/bayesian-anomaly-detection)

An exploration of hierarchical Bayesian modeling for rare-event anomaly detection.

`Python` · `PyMC` · `Bayesian Statistics` · `Probabilistic Modeling`

Uses entity-specific probabilistic baselines rather than global classification thresholds to identify statistically unusual behavior.
